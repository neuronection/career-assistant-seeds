# Code of conduct

Be respectful and constructive. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing and deliberate disruption are not acceptable in issues, pull requests, discussions or any project space.

Keep contributions factual and licensed: no personal data, no content you do not have the right to redistribute, and no vendor promotion disguised as a data correction.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to **seeds@neuronection.com**. Maintainers may edit, remove or reject contributions and may restrict participation to protect the project. Reports are handled confidentially.
